How It Works

How the PDF is generated

Entirely in your browser. No data leaves your device.

When you click Download PDF in BillGST, the application uses jsPDF — a JavaScript library that runs entirely in your browser — to compose the invoice layout and convert it to a PDF file.


The PDF is saved directly to your device's download folder. No data is uploaded to a server, and no third-party service is involved in generating the file.


You can print the invoice directly from the PDF, or attach it to an email. The layout is designed to print correctly on A4 paper.

FAQ

Questions about invoice PDFs

Can I download the GST invoice as a PDF?

Yes. Once your invoice is complete, click the Download PDF button on the invoice page. The PDF is saved directly to your device.

Is my invoice data sent to a server when generating the PDF?

No. The PDF is generated entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your invoice data is not uploaded anywhere during this process.

Can I print the invoice?

Yes. You can print directly from BillGST using the Print button, which opens your browser's print dialog with a clean invoice layout. You can also open the downloaded PDF in any PDF viewer and print from there.

What paper size does the invoice use?

The invoice is designed for A4 paper, which is the standard for business documents in India.
