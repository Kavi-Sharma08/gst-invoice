What Goes Into a Valid GST Invoice?

If your business is registered under GST in India, you are required to issue a tax invoice for each taxable supply. This guide explains what a GST invoice is, which fields are mandatory, and what CGST, SGST, IGST, HSN, and SAC actually mean.

Published by BillGST · Updated September 2026

What is a GST invoice?

A GST invoice — formally called a tax invoice — is a document issued by a GST-registered supplier to a buyer when a taxable supply of goods or services takes place. It records what was supplied, how much it costs, and how much GST is charged.

Under the CGST Act 2017, a registered taxable person must issue a tax invoice when making a taxable supply. The invoice serves as the basis for the buyer to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC), so accuracy matters.

Note on Input Tax Credit: The buyer can only claim ITC if they hold a valid tax invoice issued by a registered supplier. Errors or missing fields on the invoice can affect ITC claims.

Who needs to issue a GST invoice?

Any business registered under GST that makes a taxable supply of goods or services. Composition scheme taxpayers issue a Bill of Supply instead of a tax invoice, and cannot charge GST on it.

If you are a freelancer, consultant, or small business registered under regular (non-composition) GST, you need to issue a proper tax invoice.

Mandatory fields on a GST invoice

Rule 46 of the CGST Rules specifies the mandatory information that must appear on a tax invoice. BillGST includes all of the following fields.

What is a GSTIN?

GSTIN stands for Goods and Services Tax Identification Number. It is a unique 15-character alphanumeric code assigned to every registered GST taxpayer in India.

The format of a GSTIN is:

  • Characters 1–2: State code (e.g. 27 for Maharashtra, 07 for Delhi)
  • Characters 3–12: PAN of the taxpayer
  • Character 13: Entity number (1–9 or A–Z, for multiple registrations in the same state)
  • Character 14: Always 'Z'
  • Character 15: Check digit

BillGST checks the format of a GSTIN you enter — structure, length, and state code — but it does not connect to government records to verify whether the GSTIN is currently active. To verify an active GST registration, use the official GST portal search.

CGST, SGST, and IGST — what's the difference?

CGST and SGST (intra-state supply)

When the supplier and the buyer are in the same state, GST is split between the Centre and the State. Half goes to the Central government as CGST, and the other half goes to the State government as SGST.

Example: An 18% GST invoice for an intra-state transaction becomes 9% CGST + 9% SGST.

IGST (inter-state supply)

When the supplier and the buyer are in different states, the full GST rate is charged as IGST (Integrated GST). The Centre collects it and distributes the state's share separately.

Example: An 18% GST invoice for an inter-state transaction becomes 18% IGST.

BillGST automatically applies the correct tax type based on the states you enter for your business and your customer.

What are HSN and SAC codes?

HSN — for goods

HSN stands for Harmonised System of Nomenclature. It is an internationally standardised system used to classify traded goods. Under GST, goods are classified using HSN codes. The number of digits required depends on the annual turnover of the registered business.

SAC — for services

SAC stands for Services Accounting Code. It is India's system for classifying services under GST. Every type of service has an SAC code. For example, the SAC for IT software services is 998313; for web design, it is 998314.

If you provide professional services, consulting, design, software, or similar work, you will use SAC codes on your invoice — not HSN codes. BillGST supports both HSN and SAC codes on each line item.

What is the invoice number format?

GST rules require invoice numbers to be consecutive within a financial year, and they must not repeat. A common format used in India is:

Example format: INV/26-27/001
Where 26-27 is the financial year and 001 is the sequential number.

GST slab rates

India has five GST rate slabs:

  • 0% — Exempt or nil-rated goods and services
  • 5% — Essential goods and some services
  • 12% — A range of goods and services
  • 18% — Standard rate, commonly applied to professional services
  • 28% — Luxury goods and certain specified items

The correct rate for your specific goods or services is determined by government GST schedules. If you are unsure which rate applies to your work, consult the CBIC GST rates page or a tax professional.

Common mistakes on GST invoices

  • Missing or incorrect GSTIN — The buyer cannot claim ITC without a valid supplier GSTIN on the invoice.
  • Wrong place of supply — Determines whether CGST+SGST or IGST applies. A mistake here means tax goes to the wrong authority.
  • Non-sequential invoice numbers — GST rules require consecutive numbering within a financial year. Gaps or restarts can cause issues during GST return filing.
  • Missing HSN/SAC codes — Required based on your annual turnover. Check current thresholds on the GST portal.
  • Arithmetic errors — Manually calculated invoices are prone to errors. BillGST calculates all taxes automatically.
  • Incorrect state codes — GSTIN starts with a state code. A wrong state code means the GSTIN is structurally invalid.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a GST invoice?

A GST invoice (tax invoice) is a document issued by a GST-registered supplier to a buyer for a taxable supply of goods or services. It shows what was supplied, the taxable value, the GST rate, and the tax amount. The buyer uses it to claim Input Tax Credit.

Is a GST invoice the same as a bill?

In everyday usage, "GST bill" and "GST invoice" refer to the same document. Technically under GST law, a "Bill of Supply" is issued by composition scheme taxpayers who do not charge GST. A "Tax Invoice" is issued by regular taxpayers who charge GST. BillGST generates tax invoices for regular GST-registered businesses.

What is the difference between HSN and SAC codes?

HSN (Harmonised System of Nomenclature) codes classify goods. SAC (Services Accounting Code) codes classify services. If you supply goods, use HSN. If you supply services, use SAC. BillGST supports both on each invoice line item.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

For intra-state transactions (supplier and buyer in the same state), GST splits into CGST (Central) and SGST (State) at equal rates. For inter-state transactions (different states), the full GST rate is charged as IGST. BillGST determines which to apply based on the states you enter.

Does BillGST verify that a GSTIN is active?

No. BillGST validates the format and structure of a GSTIN — the correct character pattern, length (15 characters), and state code. It does not connect to government databases to verify whether a GSTIN is currently registered and active. To verify active registration, use the official GST portal.

Do I need to file this invoice with the GST portal?

BillGST generates your invoice document. GST return filing (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, etc.) is a separate process done directly on the GST portal or through a GST return filing tool. BillGST does not file returns on your behalf.
