Process

How to create a GST invoice

Four steps. No complicated setup.

1

Set up your business

Enter your business name, GSTIN, address, and state once in Settings. This information appears at the top of every invoice you create.

2

Add your customer

Enter or select your customer's name, GSTIN, and billing state. BillGST uses the states to determine whether CGST/SGST or IGST applies.

3

Add line items

Add the services or goods, with HSN/SAC codes, quantities, and rates. Select the applicable GST slab (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%). Tax totals are calculated instantly.

4

Download the PDF

Click Download PDF. The invoice is generated in your browser and saved to your device. No data is sent to a server.

GST Tax Types

CGST, SGST and IGST explained

BillGST determines the right tax type based on your state and your customer's state.

Tax Type When it applies How it's split
CGST + SGST Supplier and customer are in the same state (intra-state) GST rate is split equally — half to Centre (CGST), half to State (SGST)
IGST Supplier and customer are in different states (inter-state) Full GST rate applied as Integrated GST (collected by Centre)
GST Slabs Depends on goods/services category 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%

BillGST automatically applies CGST+SGST or IGST based on the states you enter. For questions about which GST rate applies to your goods or services, consult the CBIC GST portal or a qualified tax professional.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I create a GST invoice without signing up?

Yes. BillGST's core invoice creation workflow does not require an account. Open the app and start creating invoices immediately. Your data is saved to your browser's local storage.

Where is my invoice data stored?

In your browser's IndexedDB storage on this device. BillGST does not sync your data to any server. If you clear your browser data, your invoices will be deleted — so export a backup regularly.

Does BillGST verify my GSTIN against government records?

No. BillGST checks the structure and format of a GSTIN (15 characters, correct pattern), but it does not connect to government systems to verify whether the registration is active. Format validation is not the same as verifying GST registration status.

Can I use BillGST for services (not just goods)?

Yes. Each line item supports HSN codes (for goods) and SAC codes (for services). BillGST works for consultants, freelancers, and service-based businesses.

What GST rates does BillGST support?

0% (exempt/nil rated), 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. You choose the applicable rate for each line item on your invoice.

Can I back up my invoices?

Yes. Go to Settings → Backup & Restore and click Download JSON Backup. This exports all your invoices, customers, and business profiles to a JSON file you can store safely.
